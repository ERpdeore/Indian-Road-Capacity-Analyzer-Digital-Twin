For the best detection
  • Look along the road, not across it — the app maps image width to carriageway width
  • Frame the shot so the two carriageway edges (kerbs) sit at the left and right borders
  • Hold the camera level, ~1.5 m high, from the road edge or median
  • Obstruction widths are estimates — perspective is not corrected
